王翀

Professor and Head of the Department of Information Systems. I study how information technologies shape the ways people create, share, and use information in markets, organizations, and everyday life.

PhD, Information Systems, HKUST MSc Finance, Tsinghua BSc Applied Mathematics, Peking University

Research interests

Questions at the meeting point of information economics, digital platforms, and social behavior.

Social media & networks

How online friendship, identity, and influence change ratings, participation, and the public goods of digital communities.

Platforms & markets

Mechanism design in crowdsourcing, crowdfunding, auctions, and live streaming — and how small design choices move large outcomes.

FinTech & data

Financial information technology, cryptocurrency markets, and the economics of data sponsorship, assets, and digital services.

Privacy & governance

Peer privacy, data governance, and the social contracts that make a digital economy livable.

AI & human judgment

How people work with algorithms — from prediction displays to training, aversion, and visual cognition.

Methods

Analytical models, econometrics, field and lab experiments, network analysis, and data mining, used together rather than in isolation.
