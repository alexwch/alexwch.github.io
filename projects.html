Study 01

The Mischievous vs. The Disciplined

2.0 Hz
1.0 ×

This visual experiment tests human cognitive attention and visual tracking patterns. It contrasts chaotic, unpredictable stimuli (the mischievous dot) against structured, predictable movement (the disciplined dot) to observe which motion type dominates visual capture and cognitive focus.
