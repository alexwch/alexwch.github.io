In progress

Ongoing projects

Current lines of work sit at the edges of platform design, social influence, privacy, and human–AI collaboration. Titles below reflect public working papers and recent conference streams.

Human–AI

Does training induce AI aversion?

An experimental study of how people respond to algorithms after they have been trained to work with them — and when familiarity breeds distance rather than trust.

Identity & trust

Beauty and trust in digital portraits

How photo enhancement changes perceived trustworthiness, and what that means for identity, markets, and the camera as a social instrument.

Platforms

Rapid release and third-party updating

An empirical look at how a platform’s release cadence — using Firefox as a setting — shapes the update behavior of complementary apps.

Social networks

Friend recommendation in the wild

A field experiment on whether “people you may know” features change network formation, community participation, and the texture of online friendship.

Privacy

Privacy-preserving contracts

Designing agreements that keep the informational value of data in play without asking people to give themselves away.

Live streaming

Bilateral relationships on live platforms

A power-dependence view of creator–audience ties, loyalty, and the long tail of creator ecosystems.

Learning

Guidance, interaction, and digital learning

Field-experimental work on when behavioral guidance and interactive features help — or crowd out — online learning.

Opinion

Polarization in directed and undirected talk

How the architecture of a conversation — one-way or two-way — changes sentiment, self-selection, and the weather of public opinion.
