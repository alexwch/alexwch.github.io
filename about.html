More about me

Life outside the paper

The academic year has its seasons. This page is for the quieter parts — the ones that do not show up in a CV.

Home & city

I live and work in Hong Kong, a city that rewards walking and looking. Earlier years were spent in Beijing and in graduate school at HKUST. The through-line is a preference for rooms with good light, unhurried meals, and enough quiet to think.

Tea, walking, reading

I like the small rituals that reset a day: tea, a long walk, a book that is not assigned. Design that is spare without being cold — Japanese and Nordic rooms, wood, paper, stone — is the visual language I keep returning to.

Making & looking

Photography, especially of ordinary streets and interiors, is a hobby that sits next to research more closely than it first appears: both are about what people reveal when they think nobody is measuring them.

Music & rest

Evenings are for music that does not demand a thesis, time with family, and the unglamorous work of keeping a life that can support the next question.
